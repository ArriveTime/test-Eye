Overview of Vault Services & Protocols

Our zero-knowledge vault is engineered for high-net-worth individuals, tech founders, and estate planners who require bank-grade privacy and guaranteed digital inheritance.

Estate & Dead-Man's Switch Protocol

1. Zero Privacy Compromise Today

Your assets remain completely zero-knowledge encrypted. Beneficiaries have zero access or visibility while you are active.

2. Automated Activity Monitoring

The vault tracks your status via periodic cryptographic check-ins (e.g., every 90, 180, or 365 days) via email or hardware security keys.

3. Grace Period & Fail-Safe Alerts

If a check-in is missed, a multi-stage countdown triggers fail-safe alerts to your fallback contacts to prevent accidental release.

4. Encrypted Transfer to Beneficiaries

If unverified past the grace period, decryption keys release directly to your designated beneficiaries, executors, or legal trustees.
